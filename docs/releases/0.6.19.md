# English patch 0.6.19 — PS3 / RPCS3

For the Japanese retail PS3 version of Super Robot Wars Z3: Jigoku-hen
(BLJS10256). Requires your own game dump. Downloads contain ISO patches,
not a complete game.

## What changed since 0.6.18

- **Post-save scenes are translated.** After you save, a cast member comments
  on your session, usually with a voice clip. All 94 scenes are now in English.
  Where the clip is a battle line, the text matches that line's battle subtitle.
- **Team Setup and Sub Orders screens** are translated: menu items, key hints,
  the Basic/Balance Order popup, header hints, rename messages, the four
  Sub Orders and their result messages. Highlighted words now line up over
  their English.
- **Custom Bonus popup** no longer overlaps. **Every Ace, Custom and Full
  Upgrade bonus description** (236) is now in English.
- **Numbered mission conditions** no longer show their second line in
  Japanese. The continuation starts under "1." rather than under the text.
- **Spirit command names follow Akurasu:** 不屈 is now **Wall** (was Persist)
  and 鉄壁 is **Guard** (was Wall). Tessa's line now says "Alert" rather than
  "Flash".
- Dialogue: "Guren Mk-II" in Asuka's line.

Still Japanese: preset team names (e.g. エヴァンゲリオン, グレン団) and the series
names burned into the opening movie. Team names already stored in a save stay
as they are until renamed.

## RPCS3

Close the emulator, extract the verified image's `PS3_GAME` folder with an ISO
tool (or boot the ISO directly), and back up your previous installation.
With RPCS3 CLOSED, move `dev_hdd0/game/BLJS10256_DATA` to a backup outside
`dev_hdd0/game` so the game rebuilds its data on first boot. Never remove
`dev_hdd0/home/.../savedata` or other games' folders.

## Modified PS3 (CFW with fake-SELF support)

The executable is a debug/fake SELF, **not a retail signature**. It needs CFW
with fake-SELF support (e.g. Cobra) and does not work on unmodified retail
firmware. Load the ISO through your backup manager as usual.

**Hardware status:** the executable packaging matches a build confirmed booting
on a modified PS3 (0.6.15). The disc layout introduced in 0.6.18 keeps
unchanged files in their original positions, which keeps the patches small.
**That layout has not been boot-tested on hardware yet**; please report
whether it boots.
